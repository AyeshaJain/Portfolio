Brick Rodeo

Brick Rodeo mockups for DECA's integrated marketing campaign.

Goal

We wanted to strengthen Brick Rodeo's brand and expand its community through strategic partnerships, improved digital outreach, deeper STEM involvement, and website improvements. We did this by focusing on how our campaign can increase social media reach by 30%, expand STEM engagement by 25%, raise $35,000 for Dell Children's Hospital, reduce bounce rates by 15%, and promote the brand slogan: "Building a Community Brick by Brick."

Approach

We researched our audience first: males and females ages 14–34 with $86K–$111K household income, engaged in STEM and eager to showcase their own builds and add activities on their resume for college, concentrated in Texas's biggest metros (Houston, Dallas, Fort Worth, The Woodlands, Sugar Land). We backed our approaches to our campaign KPIs through various studies (86% of businesses say community management drives success; visual donation trackers can lift giving by 30%; radio recall boosts ad clicks 200%; etc…). Wanting to creating a catchy tag-line that Brick Rodeo lacked, we positioned the campaign around "Building a Community, Brick by Brick" and built a 6-week campaign plan. It followed through this timeline (teasers → radio/digital ads → STEM outreach → partnerships → hosting competitions and online fundraisers → SEO/UX fixes → restructuring and keyword-targeted subpages for SEO, and a CDN plus simplified mobile layout to cut bounce rate → measure success through data collection through coding qualitative data).

Deliverables

  • Social: #BrickbyBrick collaborative-build hashtag campaign, Instagram promo posts/newsletter pushes
  • STEM competition: "Build with a Purpose" LEGO robot challenge (high schoolers only), online registration, event flyer, certificates + custom trophy, winners featured on-site
  • Fundraising: YouTube livestream with real-time donation goal bar, continued charity auction, per-booth donation boxes, Dell Children's-branded T-shirts ($15 each)
  • Paid media: Texas radio spots (KUTX, 99.3, 99.7, 93.1, KTSA 550 AM), digital display ads (~100K impressions)
  • Physical: STEM pamphlets distributed to high schools
  • Website: SEO restructure, bounce-rate fixes (CDN, mobile layout, top-of-page nav), accessibility upgrades, donate-page redesign
  • Budget: ~$28,970 across ads, site redesign, LEGO bricks/figurines, bot challenges, T-shirts, and pamphlets, against a minimum $120K ticket-sales baseline (31% projected ROI)

Young Yale Global Scholars

An independent Instagram marketing post created to promote the Young Yale Global Scholars program.

Goal

My main goal was to create a unique attention grabbing social media post for Young Yale Global Scholar's Instagram modeled after Yale Public Health's Instagram. I worked under Yale Marketing director Melissa Minikowski to fufil her vision for the Instagram post as well.

Approach

First I studied Yale Public Health's Instagram post to understand what category of items they included and what color schemes and aesthetics they included. Afterwards, I went on Pinterest and created a collage for each sector of YYGS and then cut and pasted them into Canva to organize. I noticed that YYGS had a color scheme (red, blue, green) and Yale PH (Public Health) incorporated famous trios and duos in their post, so I decided to utilize the PowerPuff Girls which fell into both categories. After finalizing all the design aspects, I sent it over to my supervisor!

Anblicks

A LinkedIn campaign built using AI-powered marketing strategies for Anblick's AI Factory Construct and ReplatformAI

Goal

My goal as a marketing intern was to support Anblicks' marketing team in launching LinkedIn ad campaigns for ReplatformAI and AI Factory Construct, building product and brand understanding for prospective LinkedIn customers wanting to schedule a meeting with the company. In addition, I worked on sharpening my ad copywriting skills and messaging in order to successfully produce at least one live campaign.

Approach

For my research, I used Anblick's websites promoting their two services and took a week to fully understand the marketing funnel the company went by, including who their target market audience was and if they had any competitors to make a comprehensive sheets list. Afterwards, I was told by my department and manager to use Claude and Figma throughout production for efficient ad-copy production: drafting and iterating ad copy variations across five messaging themes (strategic value, stat-led, governance, cost efficiency). I made sure to fact-check claims against the product documentation, and generated visual mockups as a rough concept for the design team, not to replace any departments tasks but to accelerate the processes.

Results

  • Cost Efficiency: Delivered 88,289 total impressions on a total spend of $417.73, resulting in an economical CPM of $4.73.
  • Audience Engagement: Maintained a high average dwell time of 16.73 seconds across all ad creatives, reflecting strong message retention and visual hook effectiveness.
  • Top Performing Creative: Ad_1_Days_Weeks served as the primary growth engine, capturing 62% of campaign impressions (54,795) and driving 80% of overall landing page clicks (32 clicks) --> (ad on the bottom left).
  • Creative Testing: Ad_9_26Jun2026 achieved the highest audience attention span with an average dwell time of 19.88 seconds (ad on the bottom right).

Dior Mockups

A few of my selected works showcasing culturally localized brand imaging concepts for Dior.

* important: All mock up concepts are original. Above image has utilized Adobe Firefly to position my original work in Shanghai's subway station. Any use of AI will be credited. No AI generation alteration was used in the works of my raw brand imaging concepts.

About this portfolio: I am deeply interested in the cultural positioning of Dior lines. As a passion project, I wanted to experiment with the different concepts I could use to position Lady Dior in Shanghai, Mumbai, and New York. I researched brand ambassadors, cultural scenery, and the themes the Lady Dior campaign follows, in addition to creative collaborations Dior has already done with local artists and brands. I found that localizing marketing strategies can, in turn, provide a sense of connection as I found I recognized the brand ambassadors and collaborators for India. Sonam Kapoor, India's ambassador, reminded me of my late nights spent with my dad watching Bollywood movies. The background I chose for Sonam, which is the 5th mock up in the portfolio, rekindled my time spent wandering the historic locations in Mumbai. This connection is what I aimed to provide in each mock up, challenging myself to keep the same Dior font, yet diversifying the background, models, and bags to fit each place I'm trying to represent.

For the softwares I used, I used Canva (Pro) and Adobe as both softwares are beginner friendly, allowing me to transfer my work directly into traditional mock ups with ease. For the backgrounds, I used the website Accidentally Wes Anderson as the publicly provided photos emanate a similar aesthetic that Dior currently still uses, especially for Lady Dior. Some of the beginning backgrounds have been used from previous Dior issues so I could grasp how I could utilize existing material from Dior as well.

As I continue this project, I hope to expand my hard, creative directing, and also my cultural positioning skills in order to create successful campaigns in the future for brands alike to Dior.
